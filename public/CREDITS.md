# Lumencraft credits

All block, item and mob textures, models, terrain, shaders and code are original and generated in code.

## Sound effects — CC0 1.0 (public domain)
Recorded sound effects by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), from the packs
*Impact Sounds*, *RPG Audio*, *Foley Sounds*, *Interface Sounds* and *Sci-fi Sounds*.
Converted to mono MP3. Files are in `sfx/`.

## Piano — CC-BY 3.0
Music is generated live from samples of the **Salamander Grand Piano V3**
by Alexander Holm (https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0. Files are in `music/`.

## Fonts
Pixelify Sans and Silkscreen from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License).
